# ShipKit — Marketing Plan

**Positioning Statement:**

**Taglines:**

- "5 minutes to deploy. 5 weeks saved."

## 2. Channel Strategy

### Channel 1: Indie Hackers (High Priority)

**Why:** 500K+ active indie hackers building SaaS products. This IS your audience.

**Tactics:**
- **Build in public thread:** Daily updates during the 14-day build. "Building ShipKit Day 1/14: Auth + Stripe wired up. Here's the code."
- **Launch post:** "I built ShipKit in 14 days — the SaaS starter that skips 2 weeks of boilerplate. Here's how it works + my revenue projections." (2,000+ upvotes typical for good build-in-public)
- **Reply farming:** Find every "how do I build a SaaS" thread and mention ShipKit. Genuinely helpful, not spammy.
- **Case studies:** "I built [thing] in a weekend with ShipKit" — pay users $100 for a case study

**Schedule:** 2 posts/week on Indie Hackers
**Expected traffic:** 5,000-10,000 visitors/month within 3 months
**Conversion rate:** 3-5% to paid (IH audience has purchase intent)

### Channel 2: Product Hunt (Launch Spike)

**Why:** Best single-day traffic source for dev tools. Can generate 5,000+ visitors in 24 hours.

**Tactics:**
- **Pre-launch (2 weeks before):** Build relationships with PH makers. Join the PH maker community. Upvote other launches. Comment genuinely.
- **Launch day:** 
  - Post at 12:01 AM PT
  - First 30 minutes: Share in Indie Hackers + Twitter + Discord communities (no links to PH, just "check the front page")
  - First hour: Respond to every comment within 15 minutes
  - Hour 2-4: Get maker friends to upvote and comment
  - Hour 6: Post on Hacker News "Show HN: ShipKit"
  - All day: Thank every supporter personally
- **Post-launch:** Announce the PH result everywhere. "We hit #3 Product of the Day!"

**Schedule:** One major launch (Month 3), one v2 launch (Month 8)
**Expected traffic:** 3,000-8,000 visitors on launch day
**Conversion rate:** 2-4% (PH crowds are curious, not always buying)

### Channel 3: Hacker News (High Risk, High Reward)

**Why:** Best organic traffic source for developer tools. A front-page "Show HN" can bring 10,000+ visitors.

**Tactics:**
- **The "Show HN" post:** "Show HN: ShipKit – A SaaS starter that deploys in 5 minutes with auth, billing, and a dashboard"
  - Best timing: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM ET
  - First comment should explain what makes it different (not just what it is)
  - Don't be promotional — HN hates marketing speak
- **The "Ask HN" strategy:** On slower days, pose questions to Hacker News that indirectly promote ShipKit:
  - "Ask HN: What SaaS boilerplate takes you the longest to build?"
  - "Ask HN: What would you pay for a managed SaaS starter kit?"
- **Comment engagement:** Reply to every "Show HN" for similar tools, every thread about "best SaaS stack 2026"

**Schedule:** "Show HN" on launch day, "Ask HN" once/week
**Expected traffic:** 3,000-15,000 visitors per front-page appearance

### Channel 4: Twitter/X (Ongoing Presence)

**Why:** 200K+ indie hackers and solo founders active on Twitter. Low-effort, high-engagement.

**Tactics:**
- **Build in public:** Tweets like "Day 8/14 of building ShipKit. The landing page builder is live 👇 [screenshot]"
- **Value threads:** "The 5 SaaS infra decisions every solo founder makes wrong (and what to do instead)"
- **Reply to trending SaaS threads:** Any "What stack should I use for my SaaS?" thread → 1 tweet about ShipKit
- **DM outreach:** Find accounts saying "building a SaaS" → send a useful DM (not a pitch)
- **Twitter Spaces:** Host "Solo SaaS Office Hours" weekly
- **Growth loop:** Every user who launches with ShipKit gets a shareable launch card that links back

**Schedule:** 2-3 tweets/day, 1 thread/week, 1 Spaces/week
**Expected followers:** 500 (month 3) → 2,000 (month 6) → 5,000 (month 12)

### Channel 5: LinkedIn (Thought Leadership)

**Why:** Lower volume than Twitter but higher quality leads. Enterprise buyers are on LinkedIn.

**Tactics:**
- **Long-form posts:** "How I built a SaaS from scratch in 14 days (with $0 funding)"
- **Articles:** "The indie hacker's guide to launching a SaaS in 2026" (LinkedIn Articles)
- **Case studies:** Tag early users in case study posts
- **Creator mode:** Turn on LinkedIn creator mode with "SaaS Development" as topic

**Schedule:** 3 posts/week, 1 article/month
**Expected engagement:** 500-2,000 impressions per post

## 6. Viral Loops (Built-in Growth Engine)

ShipKit has **3 built-in viral loops**:

### Loop 1: Referral Mechanics (in the growth engine)
- Every user gets a referral link
- Refer 3 friends → 1 month free
- Refer 10 friends → 3 months free
- Refer 30 friends → 1 year free
- Referrals show "Referred by [user]" — social proof + tracking

### Loop 2: "Built with ShipKit" Badge
- Every generated SaaS footer can include "Built with ShipKit"
- Clicking the badge links back → free traffic
- Option to remove badge (Pro/Enterprise)

### Loop 3: Launch Announcement Cards
- When a user deploys their SaaS, ShipKit auto-generates a "I just launched [app] built with ShipKit" social card
- One-click share to Twitter/LinkedIn → free impressions
- Card includes "Powered by ShipKit"

## 8. Key Takeaway

ShipKit's marketing strategy is **content-first, community-driven**:

- **50% of acquisition:** Indie Hackers + Hacker News + Product Hunt (free, high-conversion)
- **30% of acquisition:** SEO + blog content (compounds over time)
- **15% of acquisition:** Word of mouth + referrals (built-in viral loops)
- **5% of acquisition:** Paid sponsorships (accelerant, not primary)

**Total marketing budget: ~$6,500/year**
**Expected Year 1 MRR: $31,000**
**Marketing ROI: 23.4× annually**
